# v3.2 Interaction RC1｜High-Risk QUICK vs FULL Stress Test

> Test type: single-window controlled simulation. This is not an external multi-agent blind test.
> Goal: determine whether QUICK can remain concise without becoming overconfident on high-stakes enterprise decisions.
> Rule: use question stem only. Do not use benchmark reality outcome when generating QUICK/FULL answers.

## Test set

1. Q012｜一家电器厂负债7000多万、资金链断裂、220人停工，但品牌、产线和渠道都还在。应该破产清算还是继续救？
2. Q028｜公司缺钱，有资金方愿意借5000万，同时会参与董事会安排，这种钱要不要拿？
3. Q021｜准备1.74亿欧元收购一家欧洲高端铜箔厂，战略价值很大，要不要签？
4. Q074｜准备在海外设厂，当地人工便宜，但管理、合规和供应链都更复杂。便宜的人工能不能成为建厂理由？
5. Q041｜原材料价格上涨、客户回款延迟，贷款也快到期了。现在应该优先还银行、保供应商，还是保工资和生产？

## Evaluation dimensions

For each question, compare QUICK against FULL on:
- Direction consistency
- Preservation of key contradiction
- Preservation of decision-changing unknowns
- Preservation of reversal condition
- Overconfidence risk
- Unsafe compression / premature priority ordering
- Length reduction

## Per-question QUICK design

### Q012｜清算还是继续救

QUICK should NOT answer “救” or “清算” directly.

Recommended QUICK pattern:
- Judgment: do not decide by debt amount alone; first test whether going-concern value still exceeds liquidation value and whether a viable cash bridge exists.
- Real question: is there still a business worth rescuing, and can it survive long enough to realize that value?
- Critical checks: recurring gross profit / order base; minimum restart cash; creditor and supplier willingness to support restructuring.
- Reversal: if core business has no positive contribution margin or restart funding cannot be secured, shift toward orderly exit/liquidation.

Why this passes: QUICK preserves the asset-vs-going-concern distinction without pretending to know recovery value.

### Q028｜5000万资金 + 董事会安排

QUICK should not frame this as simply “interest rate vs no interest rate”.

Recommended QUICK pattern:
- Judgment: money can be attractive while control terms are expensive; evaluate the package, not the cash alone.
- Real question: what rights are being exchanged for liquidity?
- Critical checks: board appointment/veto rights; repayment and default terms; whether investor brings strategic resources beyond money.
- Reversal: if governance rights can materially constrain future financing, M&A or founder control, reject or renegotiate even if funding price is low.

Why this passes: it retains Money + Rights + Resources + Constraints in compressed form.

### Q021｜1.74亿欧元跨境并购

QUICK should not answer based on “strategic value is large”.

Recommended QUICK pattern:
- Judgment: HOLD until the acquisition thesis is converted into measurable synergies, downside exposure and integration milestones.
- Real question: are we buying durable capability and cash flow, or buying an expensive strategic story?
- Critical checks: standalone earning power; integration and technology/customer transferability; downside if synergies fail.
- Reversal: if the deal still creates value under a conservative no-synergy case or if downside is contractually bounded, confidence can rise.

Why this passes: QUICK preserves assumption audit and optionality while avoiding a fake valuation.

### Q074｜海外设厂

QUICK should reject “cheap labor = build plant”.

Recommended QUICK pattern:
- Judgment: labor cost alone is not a sufficient investment reason.
- Real question: does total landed economics plus strategic value justify a less reversible capital commitment?
- Critical checks: total cost including compliance/logistics/management; local demand certainty; ability to pilot via export/contract manufacturing before owning a plant.
- Reversal: if localized production materially improves customer access or tariff/supply-chain resilience and the economics remain positive after full-cost adjustment, the case strengthens.

Why this passes: QUICK keeps system-boundary and optionality logic.

### Q041｜现金生存期：银行/供应商/工资和生产谁优先

This is the hardest QUICK case.

QUICK must NOT produce a universal ranking such as “工资 > 供应商 > 银行”.

Required guardrail:
- Judgment: this is a cash-survival triage problem; priority depends on which payment prevents irreversible destruction of operating value or immediate legal/operational shutdown.
- Real question: which minimum payments keep the company alive for the next cash cycle?
- Critical checks: payroll/legal deadline; which supplier stoppage would halt production; bank default/freeze/cross-default consequences.
- Reversal: if any single payment prevents account freeze, license loss, worker stoppage or key supply cutoff, it temporarily jumps to the top.

Why this is PASS WITH GUARDRAIL: the short answer remains useful only if it explicitly refuses a universal priority order and asks for the three decision-changing facts.

## Interaction failures checked

- QUICK_OVERSIMPLIFICATION: not observed in Q012/Q028/Q021/Q074; controlled in Q041 with mandatory guardrail.
- QUICK_OVERCONFIDENCE: low across all five when status uses HOLD / CONDITIONAL / NEED_MORE_EVIDENCE appropriately.
- QUICK_GENERIC: acceptable; each QUICK retains the case-specific economic mechanism.
- QUICK_FULL_DRIFT: none observed in controlled simulation.
- QUICK_TOO_LONG: not observed; target remains roughly 180–350 Chinese characters for natural first-turn usage.

## Design conclusion

v3.2 Interaction RC1 remains viable, but QUICK needs one explicit safety rule for high-stakes decisions:

### HIGH_STAKES_COMPRESSION_GATE [ENGINE-C]

If the question involves any of the following:
- insolvency / restructuring / liquidation
- imminent cash-chain break
- control rights / board rights
- large M&A / irreversible investment
- legal default / freeze / shutdown risk

QUICK must:
1. avoid categorical GO/NO-GO unless direction is robust to missing facts;
2. surface up to 3 decision-changing unknowns;
3. use conditional status when needed;
4. include at least 1 reversal condition;
5. never invent a universal stakeholder priority order.

This rule changes presentation discipline, not the v3.1 decision engine.

SIMULATED PASS WITH ONE GUARDRAIL.
